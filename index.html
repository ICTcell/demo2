About

Career summary

Kinnari has spent over a decade in higher education, teaching core computer engineering subjects while steadily building a research profile in artificial intelligence and data mining. She currently manages the ICT Cell at Parul University, where she trains faculty on emerging tools — from LaTeX and Mentimeter to the university's LMS — while continuing her own Ph.D. research.

Her work spans customer behaviour analytics, text mining, facial recognition and image classification, published across international journals and conferences. She has also authored three books covering C#, C programming and data mining fundamentals.
